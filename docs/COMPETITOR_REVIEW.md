## FrameShift v9 position

FrameShift is a local, self-contained prototype with three internally defined levels, six mixed/paired practice steps, DILR ordering tasks, Quant, VARC, visual data interpretation, a basic calculator, a rule-based demo Coach and a rule-based personalised session recap. It has no backend or live AI service. Its current product hypothesis is that a *curated focus-switch sequence and differently framed transfer task* may help students apply reasoning beyond a familiar question format.

The calculator, chatbot, dark mode, confidence inputs, hints, visual charts, adaptive labels, analytics and personalised recommendations should not be claimed as unique. Competitors advertise several of these. The intended difference is the particular sequence of concept-focused practice, context switching and a fresh transfer task—but that additional learning value is unvalidated.

## Survey evidence boundary

The user-provided Google Form export had 15 submitted records and 14 valid responses for most core questions. This is a small, self-selected sample. It supports exploring relevant practice and personalised feedback, not proving population demand, adoption, learning efficacy or score improvement. Multi-select results are selection frequencies, not exclusive rankings.
