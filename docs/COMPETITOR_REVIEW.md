**Review purpose:** Identify market overlap and avoid inaccurate differentiation claims.  
**Evidence type:** Official public product pages unless otherwise stated. Feature availability and pricing can change; pages should be rechecked before an external submission.

## What can and cannot be claimed

These pages support statements about advertised capabilities, not independent conclusions about learning efficacy. Do not claim that FrameShift is the first or only product with calculators, hints, confidence ratings, mock analysis or personalised feedback.

FrameShift's current hypothesis is a compact focus-switch micro-practice flow that pairs questions with different surface framings. Whether this creates incremental learning value is unvalidated and requires tests with real aspirants.
